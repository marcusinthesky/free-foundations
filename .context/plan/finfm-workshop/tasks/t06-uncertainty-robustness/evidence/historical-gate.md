## Inference conventions

- **Bootstrap centring (anomaly):** the replication cross-section draws are shrunk
  toward zero relative to their point estimates.
  - Size 10-NN with an intercept: point estimate 0.248, draw median 0.134, percentile
    interval [−0.031, 0.252].
  - Fernández joint 10-NN: point −0.165, draw median −0.069.
  - Decision: the workshop paper does not quote the matched 10-NN cross-section table.
  - The journal roadmap asks for basic or BCa intervals and a diagnosis of the shrinkage.
- **Mantel p-values:** 999 permutations, with Holm across 12 tests. The smallest
  possible adjusted p-value is 12/1000 = 0.012, and every reported value sits at that
  floor. Quote them as "at the permutation floor", not as 0.012.
- **Confidence intervals and Holm:** reported intervals are unadjusted. Holm adjusts
  p-values only, so a CI can exclude zero while the Holm p exceeds 0.05. State this
  wherever both appear.

## Replication scope

Fernández (2011) used Latin American firms over 1997–2006. That sample is not available
here, so the CRSP–SEC study is a reimplementation of her design, not a replication. Its
differences are recorded in the paper's design table: population, frequency, variable
sources and the lease treatment.
