# Paired dependence-aware intervals

This is a proposed protocol, not an implemented or validated interval. For each prespecified formation and endpoint, define the estimand, units, comparison direction, ordered issuer roster, factor ordering, calendar, and common observation mask before examining draws. Retain period-indexed issuer outcomes, factors and endpoint contributions, plus all input and graph identities. The paired contrast is the difference between two estimates evaluated on that same population and calendar; calculate it within each draw, never by subtracting separate interval endpoints. Financial effects, PCoA alignments and pricing errors require their own estimands and refit maps; a static formation-level alignment without a period-indexed sampling representation cannot acquire a time-block interval merely by repeating a constant.

Use the original contiguous calendar at the data frequency (quarters for quarterly estimates, months for monthly estimates). Draw stationary-bootstrap blocks with replacement, wrapping at the calendar end: start uniformly, and at each next position restart uniformly with probability `1/L`, otherwise advance one period. Select and record expected block length L before contrasts, in calendar units, and repeat a prespecified shorter and longer L as sensitivity. Apply each index sequence jointly to every issuer, both compared models, factors and the observation mask, preserving cross-sectional dependence and within-block serial dependence. Do not compress missing periods before constructing blocks. The comparison requires a common finite mask and minimum overlap fixed in advance; report its dates, ordered roster, observed counts and exclusions. Missingness remains attached to its sampled period; this procedure does not correct informative exits or establish stationarity.

For a **conditional** interval freeze formation membership, checkpoints, states, preprocessing, readout, graph, loadings and other fitted nuisance quantities at their original values, and recompute only the declared outcome statistic on the resampled observations. For a **refitted** interval repeat all data-dependent financial steps relevant to that endpoint in each draw: factor/loadings and means, preprocessing and calibrated readouts where fitted from resampled financial data, model parameters, risk prices and the endpoint. Frozen checkpoint weights remain fixed. Declare whether the formation graph is fixed or reconstructed from refitted inputs; if fixed, label the result conditional on the original graph even when other stages refit. Fixing graph and nuisance quantities excludes their estimation uncertainty. Refit the original sample first and verify parity with its saved point estimate before using draw estimates; the existing 10-NN anomaly remains unresolved in [refit audit](refit.md).

Set a recorded deterministic seed, generator and version, draw count, block length, original input digests and code revision. Generate one shared index matrix per endpoint family and use identical draw IDs for every comparison. Prespecify fit validity, rank and overlap thresholds and the handling of draws where one member fails. Never independently discard failures from either arm: mark the paired draw failed, retain its reason and draw ID, and report attempted, joint-success and failure counts, including attrition and boundary or singular fits. If a draw loses issuers, retain its mask and ordered roster; any induced graph restriction and row renormalization must be identical across arms and reported. Such draws change the effective population and graph: present their sensitivity separately from a fixed-roster analysis, not as though they estimate exactly the original-roster contrast. If too few valid paired draws remain under a prespecified threshold, or failures are systematic, report the interval unavailable rather than silently conditioning on success.

For an eligible fixed estimand use the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of successful paired contrast draws as a nominal 95% percentile interval; record the quantile convention and success count. State the dependence, stability and moment conditions needed for bootstrap validity, and assess coverage in a suitable fixture before interpreting it as calibrated. Prespecify the family of related hypotheses, including all planned model contrasts and endpoints in that family. Apply Holm adjustment to valid two-sided p-values from a separately specified valid test; report raw and adjusted p-values and family membership. These marginal percentile intervals are not Holm-adjusted simultaneous intervals. Do not infer a multiplicity-adjusted test merely from interval overlap.
