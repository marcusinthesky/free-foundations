# FinFM manuscript brief

Working editorial handoff, 19 November 2026. Scope and deadlines below are proposals to the authors; evidence gates and author decisions remain open.

## Question and reader

When does a frozen tabular representation furnish an economically defensible measure of firm proximity? For FinFM's AI researchers, finance academics, practitioners and regulators, connect a formal distinction between recoverable information and identified geometry to an audited, in-sample US reimplementation of Fernández. Present frozen features as candidate measurement instruments, with an explicit financial estimation inventory. Do not promise forecasting, comparative sample efficiency, causal transmission, no-arbitrage pricing, or interpretable factors without their separate evidence. Retain null and negative findings; an unavailable test is unidentified, and nonsignificance is not equivalence.
