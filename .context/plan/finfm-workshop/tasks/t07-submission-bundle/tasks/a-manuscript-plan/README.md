# 07a Plan the manuscript and page budget

## Goal

Start from the reviewed introduction and the paragraph comments in all eight shared section files. Own the editorial brief at `evidence/manuscript-plan.md` in this task. The brief is the handoff to all section writers.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Verify submission-form fields against the official call. Record the deadline, anonymity rules and author decisions early. FinFM allows seven technical pages for a long paper, with additional pages for references and appendices. Supplementary material is optional. [FinFM call](https://finfm.finhorizons.org/)
- [ ] Specify the workshop question, contribution, intended reader and paragraph purpose for each section. Budget technical pages including floats. Assign each planned claim, theorem and exhibit to its research owner and a main-text or appendix slot.
- [ ] Choose the smallest supported paper if results arrive late. Mark claims with missing evidence for qualification, omission or the journal roadmap. Record author feedback on the scope before finalizing the framing.
