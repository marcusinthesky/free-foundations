# Related-work source and citation handoff

Checked 19 November 2026 against the primary works linked below. Keys already exist in the project BibTeX export and canonical CSL records. The draft uses only these five keys. The technical interpretation and study-specific claims remain subject to the manuscript and evidence audit.

| Key | Primary source and verified metadata | Claim supported and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fernandez2011` | Viviana Fernández, “Spatial linkages in international financial markets,” *Quantitative Finance* 11(2), 237–245 (2011), [DOI 10.1080/14697680903127403](https://doi.org/10.1080/14697680903127403). | Spatial CAPM and financial-characteristic measures of nongeographic proximity. The new US study is a reimplementation, not evidence about the original sample. |
| `kou2018` | Steven Kou, Xianhua Peng and Haowen Zhong, “Asset Pricing with Spatial Interaction,” *Management Science* 64(5), 2083–2101 (2018), [DOI 10.1287/mnsc.2016.2627](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2016.2627). Publisher notes online publication in 2017 and volume date 2018. | Spatial CAPM and S-APT; applications to eurozone indices and housing futures. An estimated covariance pattern alone is not a test of all pricing premises. |
| `gu2021` | Shihao Gu, Bryan Kelly and Dacheng Xiu, “Autoencoder asset pricing models,” *Journal of Econometrics* 222(1), 429–450 (2021), [DOI 10.1016/j.jeconom.2020.07.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2020.07.009). | Characteristic-dependent exposures and latent factors in a financially trained autoencoder; its out-of-sample pricing evaluation is not evidence for this paper's frozen models. |
| `hollmann2025` | Noah Hollmann et al., “Accurate predictions on small data with a tabular foundation model,” *Nature* 637, 319–326 (2025), [DOI 10.1038/s41586-024-08328-6](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08328-6). | TabPFN learns across synthetic datasets and supports prediction and reusable embeddings. Its evaluation does not validate financial proximity. Canonical CSL abbreviates authors as “others”; publisher lists eight authors, so bibliography owner should decide whether to expand it. |
| `roeder2021` | Geoffrey Roeder, Luke Metz and Diederik P. Kingma, “On Linear Identifiability of Learned Representations,” *Proceedings of the 38th ICML*, PMLR 139, 9030–9039 (2021), [paper and proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/roeder21a.html). | A family of discriminative models is identifiable up to linear indeterminacy under assumptions. The non-isometric distance observation is an elementary implication, not a claim that all learned representations satisfy that theorem. Proceedings landing-page citation metadata spells the third author “Durk Kingma,” while the paper PDF names Diederik P. Kingma, as in the existing CSL record. |
