# FinFM submission cover note

**For Marcus and Chun-Sung.** The anonymous long-paper candidate is
*Pretrained Tabular Features and the Measurement of Financial Proximity*.
The [bundle task](tasks/t07-submission-bundle/README.md) links the review and
submission materials. The [official call](https://finfm.finhorizons.org/)
extends the deadline to **23 November 2026 at 23:59 AoE**,
equivalent to **24 November at 13:59 SAST**.

The paper asks when a frozen tabular encoder improves on peers chosen from
the same financial records. It compares factor-adjusted return fits, peer
weighting rules and four encoders, then examines how many of the selected
model's neighbours survive projection onto its leading valuation coordinate.
The four-firm example explains how changing coordinates can change peer
weights while preserving predictions. The [candidate handoff](tasks/t07-submission-bundle/evidence/candidate.md)
identifies the revised anonymous PDF, matching OpenReview text and
canonical evidence used for this build.
